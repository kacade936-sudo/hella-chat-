1. Overview & Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to the Hella Chat mobile application (Android package com.aistudio.pulsechat.kqmxvz) and describes how your information is processed when you sign in, send messages, post status updates, participate in communities or channels, link companion devices, or make voice and video calls.

Summary: Hella Chat uses Google Sign-In for authentication, stores your account, messages, statuses, and settings in Google Cloud Firestore scoped by security rules, supports client-side end-to-end encryption for direct messages, keeps biometric and app-lock credentials exclusively on your device, and does not sell your personal data or serve third-party behavioral advertising.

2. Information We Collect

A. Account & Profile Information

When you sign in with Google and complete onboarding, Hella Chat collects and stores:

  • Google Authentication Identifiers: Your Firebase User ID (UID), email address, email verification status, and basic Google profile name/photo provided by Google Sign-In via Jetpack Credential Manager.
  • Public Profile Details: Your chosen unique @username, display name, bio/about text, optional profile picture, online/last-seen presence state (subject to your privacy settings), and your public ECDH encryption key.
  • Account Preferences & Private Settings: Notification preferences, language choice (English, Somali, Arabic), theme mode, wallpaper/bubble customizations, blocked/muted/archived/pinned conversation lists, optional recovery email, and optional hashed 6-digit Two-Step Verification PIN.

B. Contacts, Chats, Statuses, Communities & Channels

  • Messages & Shared Media: Text messages, emojis, stickers, GIFs, voice recordings, images, videos, documents, reply/forward metadata, reactions, and read/delivery receipts stored in Cloud Firestore conversations.
  • User-Added Contacts & Phone Lookup Index: Contacts you explicitly look up or add by username or phone number. Phone numbers indexed for discovery use one-way cryptographic SHA-256 hashing so raw phone numbers are not exposed in the public lookup index.
  • Statuses (24-Hour Expiry): Text, photo, video, collage layout, or voice status updates you post, along with viewer lists and timestamps according to your chosen status privacy audience.
  • Communities & Channels: Community names, descriptions, membership/admin lists, group memberships, public or private channel metadata, posts, and follower counts.
  • User Reports: Moderation reports you submit (reported user ID, optional conversation ID, and reason).

C. Voice & Video Calls and Linked Devices

  • Call Metadata & Signaling: Call type (voice or video), participant IDs, timestamps, call status, call duration, and transient WebRTC signaling data (SDP offers/answers and ICE candidates) required to connect peer-to-peer calls.
  • Linked Devices: Device name, platform type, device ID, pairing session codes, and last active timestamps when you link additional devices via QR code pairing.

D. Local Device-Only Data

  • Encryption Private Keys: Your ECDH private key pair is generated and stored locally in private app preferences on your device and is never uploaded to Firestore.
  • App Lock Credentials: Pattern, PIN, or Password lock secrets are stored locally on your device as salted SHA-256 hashes. Fingerprint verification relies strictly on Android's system BiometricPrompt API; Hella Chat never accesses or stores raw fingerprint images or biometric templates.
  • Offline Outbox & Theme/Voice Cache: Queued unsent messages when offline and temporary media playback/recording cache files are stored locally on your device.

3. How We Use Information

We process your information solely to operate, secure, and improve Hella Chat:

  • Authenticate your account securely using Google Sign-In and maintain your signed-in session (including multi-account switching).
  • Deliver real-time one-to-one chats, group conversations, community announcements, channel posts, status updates, and delivery/read receipts.
  • Establish real-time WebRTC voice and video calls and post call summaries to your conversation history.
  • Enforce per-user security controls, Two-Step Verification, blocked user filtering, disappearing message timers, and abuse reporting.
  • Provide optional user-initiated cloud chat backup and restore stored inside your private Firestore backup document.
  • Synchronize linked companion devices after explicit QR pairing confirmation.

4. Encryption & App Lock Security

  • In-Transit Protection: All communications with Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, and signaling endpoints use encrypted HTTPS/TLS connections. WebRTC media streams use standard DTLS-SRTP encryption.
  • End-to-End Encryption (Direct Messages): Hella Chat implements client-side ECDH (P-256) key agreement combined with AES-256-GCM encryption and a deterministic 60-digit safety verification number so participants can verify key fingerprints.
  • Access Control: Cloud Firestore security rules restrict private settings, contacts, backups, and linked device records to the authenticated owner UID, and restrict conversations, messages, and calls to authorized participants.
  • On-Device App Lock: You can lock Hella Chat locally with a Pattern, PIN, Password, or Fingerprint before anyone with physical access to your unlocked phone can open your chats.

5. Device Permissions

Hella Chat requests permissions on a least-privilege basis only when you use the corresponding feature:

Permission Purpose in Hella Chat Requirement
INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE Sign in with Google, sync real-time messages/statuses, and connect voice/video calls. Required (Install-time)
CAMERA Make video calls, capture photos/videos in chat, and scan QR codes for contacts or Linked Devices. Optional (Runtime)
RECORD_AUDIO & MODIFY_AUDIO_SETTINGS Record voice messages, post voice statuses, and transmit audio during voice/video calls. Optional (Runtime)
POST_NOTIFICATIONS Display alerts for incoming messages and calls on Android 13+ devices. Optional (Runtime)
USE_BIOMETRIC Verify your identity via Android BiometricPrompt when Fingerprint App Lock is enabled. Optional (User-enabled)
VIBRATE Provide notification and incoming call vibration alerts according to your settings. Optional (Normal)

Photos, Videos & Documents: Hella Chat uses the Android Photo Picker and Storage Access Framework (document picker), so you only grant access to the specific media or files you choose to share without broad storage permissions.

6. Third-Party Services & Data Sharing

Hella Chat does not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Data is shared only in these limited cases:

  • With Other Users You Communicate With: Your public profile (@username, display name, avatar, bio), messages, statuses (based on your selected visibility), group/community participation, and call requests are visible to the recipients or public channel audiences you choose.
  • Google & Firebase Infrastructure: Hella Chat uses Google Firebase Authentication (Google Sign-In), Google Cloud Firestore (real-time database and security rules), Google Play Services Credential Manager, and on-device Google ML Kit Barcode Scanning (QR processing runs locally on your device).
  • Legal & Safety Obligations: Information may be disclosed if required by applicable law, valid legal process, or to investigate abuse reports submitted through the app.

7. Data Retention & Account Deletion

  • Active Accounts: Profile data, conversations, and settings remain stored while your account is active, unless deleted by you or subject to disappearing message timers.
  • 24-Hour Statuses: Status updates are configured with a 24-hour expiration window, and owners can manually delete their statuses at any time.
  • Message Deletion: You may delete messages using Delete for Me (hides the message from your account) or Delete for Everyone (replaces message content and clears encrypted/media payloads for all participants).
  • Full Account Deletion: You can permanently delete your account from Settings → Account → Delete Account. Deleting your account removes your public user profile, private info, cloud chat backup, saved contacts, linked device records, username claim, hashed phone lookup record, and Firebase Authentication user record.

8. Your Privacy Controls

Inside Settings, you can manage at any time:

  • Visibility of your Last Seen & Online status, Profile Photo, and who can add you to Groups (Everyone, Contacts, or Nobody).
  • Read Receipts toggle and Status Privacy (My Contacts, My Contacts Except..., or Only Share With...).
  • Disappearing Messages default timer and View Once media option.
  • Blocked Contacts, Linked Devices (view and unlink sessions), Two-Step Verification PIN, and App Lock (Pattern, PIN, Password, or Fingerprint).
  • Cloud Backup creation/restoration and notification sound/preview preferences.

9. Children's Privacy

Hella Chat is not intended for children under the age of 13 (or the minimum digital consent age required in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through Hella Chat, please contact us so we can review and delete the account.
